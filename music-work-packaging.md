---
name: Music work packaging
description: Product rules for organizing contestant songs, playable versions, lyrics, and complete project bundles.
---

Treat each song/work as one library unit with one instrumental file and one original/reference file. Written lyrics are optional and may be added later.

ZIP files belong to the same work and represent the complete production package, including stems such as drums, bass, vocals, instruments, strings, and other session files. A ZIP does not replace the two playable audio versions.

Lyrics may intentionally recur across 333, 444, or other genre-specific versions; keep those versions distinguishable while allowing the written material to be shared.

**Why:** The source catalog is still being sorted, so playable pairs, lyrics, and full production bundles will become available at different times.

**How to apply:** Group and authorize by a stable work identity, label each audio version explicitly, permit lyrics to be added later, and attach any full ZIP bundle to the work rather than presenting it as audio.
